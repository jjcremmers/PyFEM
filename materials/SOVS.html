Skorohod–Olevsky Viscous Sintering

The SkorohodOlevsky model describes viscous densification and deformation of ceramic powder during sintering. It combines volumetric flow driven by sintering stress with deviatoric viscous deformation driven by applied stress. The viscosity depends on temperature and relative density, allowing the model to represent free sintering, pressure-assisted sintering, and creep during densification.

Input parameters

material =
{
  type       = "SkorohodOlevsky";
  eta0       = 1.0e12;
  Q          = 500000.0;
  T          = 1600.0;
  rho0       = 0.6;
  sigma_sint = 1.0e6;
  n_vol      = 2.0;
  n_shear    = 1.0;
  R          = 8.314;
};
