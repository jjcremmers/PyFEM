PlaneStrainDamage

PlaneStrainDamage is a two-dimensional plane-strain damage model based on linear elasticity and an equivalent-strain history variable. Damage grows irreversibly once the equivalent strain exceeds the stored maximum and reduces the effective stress and tangent stiffness.

Warning

This page was generated with the assistance of AI and must be checked carefully against the implementation and the conventions of the element being used.

Overview

The model returns S11, S22, S12, and damage. The damage variable ranges from 0 for the undamaged state to 1 for complete degradation. The response is intended for plane-strain continuum elements.
