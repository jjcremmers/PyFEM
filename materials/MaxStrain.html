MaxStrain

MaxStrain is a composite-material failure criterion that compares the normal and shear strains with allowable tensile, compressive, and shear strain limits. Failure is indicated when the largest normalized strain reaches one.

Warning

This page was generated with the assistance of AI and must be checked carefully against the implementation and the conventions of the element being used.

Failure index

For the material axes, the criterion evaluates the fiber strain, transverse strain, and in-plane shear strain independently. Tension and compression use separate limits. The failure index is the maximum normalized value; an index greater than or equal to 1 indicates failure.

Input parameters

failure =
{
  type    = "MaxStrain";
  eps1t   = 0.015;
  eps1c   = 0.012;
  eps2t   = 0.005;
  eps2c   = 0.010;
  gamma12u = 0.020;
};
