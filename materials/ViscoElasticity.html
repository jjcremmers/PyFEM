ViscoElasticity

ViscoElasticity is a time-dependent linear viscoelastic material based on a generalized Maxwell model. An equilibrium spring is combined with Maxwell elements, each having a relaxation modulus and relaxation time. The material therefore captures stress relaxation and creep in three-dimensional or two-dimensional continuum analyses.

Input parameters

material =
{
  type        = "ViscoElasticity";
  E           = 1000.0;
  nu          = 0.3;
  Einf        = 100.0;
  nMaxwell    = 2;
  relaxTimes  = [1.0, 10.0];
  relaxModuli = [300.0, 600.0];
};
